隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

应用 Application: CEVTUO Agent
生效日期 Effective date: 2026-10-21
一句话概括 / In short: 这是一个只供账号所有者本人使用的个人工具。 It is a personal tool used only by the account owner. 它读取你自己的 Pinterest 数据，只保存在你自己的电脑上，不发送给任何第三方。 It reads your own Pinterest data, stores it on your own computer, and does not send it to any third party.

1. 我们是谁 / Who we are

CEVTUO Agent 是由账号所有者自建、自用的个人助手应用，不对外提供服务，也没有其他用户。 CEVTUO Agent is a self-built, self-used personal assistant application. It is not offered as a service to the public and has no other users.

2. 访问哪些数据 / What data we access

  • 仅在你本人授权后，通过 Pinterest API v5 读取你自己账号的资料、图板（boards）与 Pin 数据。 After your explicit authorization, we read your own account's profile, boards and Pins through the Pinterest API v5.
  • 我们不读取其他 Pinterest 用户的数据，也不进行爬取或批量抓取。 We do not read other Pinterest users' data, and we do not scrape or crawl.
  • 我们不会读取你的 Pinterest 密码。授权通过 OAuth 完成，我们只持有可撤销的访问令牌。 We never see your Pinterest password. Authorization is done via OAuth; we only hold a revocable access token.

3. 数据如何存储 / How data is stored

  • 数据保存在你本人的电脑上（本地文件），不上传到我们的服务器——我们没有服务器。 Data is stored locally on your own computer. We do not upload it to any server — we have no server.
  • 访问令牌保存在本机，文件权限限制为仅当前用户可读。 The access token is stored locally with file permissions restricted to the current user.

4. 数据共享 / Data sharing

我们不出售、出租、共享或转让任何数据给第三方，也不将数据用于广告或训练模型。 We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or model training.

5. 数据保留与删除 / Retention and deletion

数据只保留在你的电脑上，你可随时删除本地文件以彻底移除。 你也可以随时在 Pinterest 账号设置中撤销本应用的授权，撤销后我们将无法再访问你的任何数据。 Data remains on your computer only; you may delete the local files at any time. You may also revoke this application's access in your Pinterest account settings at any time — after revocation we can no longer access any of your data.

6. 儿童 / Children

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其数据。 This application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
